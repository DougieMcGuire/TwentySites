What a project includes

One fixed price covers the whole job. There is no phase two where the interesting parts get quoted separately.

Writing

We start with what you actually sell and who buys it, then write the pages around that. Most clients arrive with a folder of copy nobody has read since 2019, and leave with about half as many words.

Design

Type, spacing, colour and photography, drawn for your content rather than poured into a theme. You see full pages in the browser, on a real phone, not flat pictures of pages.

Build

Written by hand — HTML, CSS and the small amount of JavaScript the page genuinely needs. It loads in under a second on a phone, and it will still run without maintenance in five years.

Handover

The repository, the hosting, the domain records and a short screen recording of how to change things. Then twelve months of small edits, included, no hourly rate attached.

How a project runs

Six weeks from the first call to a site your customers can use. Here is where the time goes and what we need from you at each point.

  1. 45 minutes

    The call

    You describe the business and what the current site gets wrong. If we are a bad fit we say so on the call and point you at someone better. If we are a good fit you get a fixed price in writing within two days.

  2. Week one

    Words and structure

    We interview you for an hour, read everything you already have, and come back with the page list and the draft copy. Nothing gets designed until you have signed off on what it says.

  3. Weeks two & three

    Design in the browser

    You get a link on Tuesday and Friday. Every page you look at is a real page at a real width, so nobody discovers in week five that the headline does not fit on a phone.

  4. Weeks four & five

    Build and content load

    We build the finished pages and move your content across — products, staff, opening hours, the lot. You review on your own phone, and we fix what you find.

  5. Week six

    Launch

    Domain, certificates, redirects from the old URLs, analytics and a search console that is actually verified. We sit on it for a week afterwards and watch what breaks.

  6. Twelve months

    The year after

    New staff photo, changed prices, a page for the second location — email us and it is done inside two working days. Included, until the year is up.

Questions we get

If yours is not here, ask it on the call. We would rather answer it before you pay us than after.

Can we edit the site ourselves afterwards?

Yes. The pages you change often — staff, prices, opening hours, products — get a plain editor with no theme settings to break. The pages you never change stay as files, which is why the site stays fast.

We already have a brand and a designer. Can you just build?

Often, yes. Send us what exists. If the design is finished and the files are usable, the build alone runs about 40% of the full price. If it is three mood boards and a logo, we should talk about designing it properly.

What do you need from us?

About six hours across six weeks: one interview, four review sessions and a final read-through. Plus one named person who can say yes. Projects run late when approval has to travel through a committee.

Do you do photography?

We do not shoot it, but we will not let you launch with stock. We brief a photographer near you and art-direct the shoot. Budget $1,200 to $2,500 for a day, paid to them.

What happens after the twelve months?

Nothing, unless you want it to. The site keeps working and you own every part of it. Edits after that are $140 an hour, or $320 a month if you would rather have us on call.

Where are you, and does it matter?

Two people, one studio, working 9 to 6 UK time. Most of the twenty we have never met in person. We will get on a plane if the project warrants it and you are paying for the seat.

Tell us what you are building.

Email is best. Say what the business does, what the site needs to achieve, and roughly when you need it live. You will hear back from one of the two of us inside a working day.
